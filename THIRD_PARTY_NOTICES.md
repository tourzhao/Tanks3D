## Optional Godot frontend and local app

`scripts/setup_godot_sample.py` downloads the official Godot 4.7.2 engine,
godot-cpp 10.0.0 bindings targeting API 4.7, and SCons 4.11.1 into `build/`.
Their source URLs, immutable revisions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`godot/DEPENDENCIES.json`. Godot and godot-cpp retain their MIT notices in
`godot/licenses/Godot-MIT.txt` and `godot/licenses/godot-cpp-MIT.txt`; Godot's
bundled component notices are in `godot/licenses/Godot-COPYRIGHT.txt`. SCons
retains its notice in `godot/licenses/SCons-MIT.txt`. These dependencies are not
relicensed under the project's PolyForm terms.

The local `build/Tanks3D-Godot.app` packages the official engine's arm64 editor
runtime and the statically linked godot-cpp binding in its engine-independent
native extension. It does not link or bundle the raylib runtime. The engine component
notices above and all
root `LICENSES/` records are retained in `Contents/Resources/licenses/`, together
with `LICENSE`, `ASSET_LICENSES.md` and this notice. Raylib and its embedded
dependencies remain used by the original application; their historical records
are retained rather than removed: `LICENSES/Zlib-raylib.txt`,
`LICENSES/Raylib-6.0-dependencies.txt` and their referenced license texts. The
complete notice collection remains external to the project PCK. Development
staging retains both texture sources, while the production Godot PCK uses only
the grass texture through its import mapping and output. The 22 OGG recordings
use the same mapping approach; raw grass PNG and OGG copies remain in development
staging rather than being duplicated inside the PCK. The local app is ad-hoc signed
and is not a notarized or attested release candidate.
SCons is a build dependency, not an application runtime. These optional targets
do not change the original raylib app's release-resource manifest.

The redesigned full vehicle roster, terrain, pickups, effects and UI in
`godot/sample/` are project-owned code and artwork under the root PolyForm
Noncommercial 1.0.0 license. Their exact source-to-runtime mapping is recorded
in `ASSET_LICENSES.md` and `docs/GODOT_ART.md`. Existing OGG files and the two
existing generated albedo textures retain their separately recorded provenance;
no SNK content or additional external art is imported for the Godot migration.
The procedural wheel/tread phase meshes are likewise original project-owned
geometry, generated by `godot/sample/art.gd`; no third-party animation or model
is imported for them.
The directional muzzle jet and bright-nosed shell in that same source are
original procedural geometry; they add no third-party effects or textures.
The cut-corner interface plates and tank emblem in `arcade_plate.gd` are
original project-owned drawing code. The aligned interface uses the same

and retain the zlib notice in `LICENSES/Zlib-raylib.txt`. They are not
relicensed as PolyForm-only content. The report preview reuses the existing
project-owned tank meshes and adds no third-party model.
The player building-occlusion shader and its runtime map mask are also original
project-owned presentation code; they reuse those same tank meshes and add no
third-party artwork or recordings.
The 22 existing OGG files are unchanged. `audio_bank.gd` consumes the original
native cue order and playback policy; the engine-neutral session extraction
