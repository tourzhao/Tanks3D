# Tanks 3D v0.1.0-alpha.1

_Classic four-direction tank combat, rebuilt as a fixed-camera isometric 3D
game for Apple Silicon Macs._

> **Release candidate:** the automated gates have passed, but this page is not
> approved for public release until exact-candidate screenshots, clean-Mac
> launch QA, and the inherited-audio decision are recorded.

Tanks 3D keeps the readable lanes and base-defense rules of classic arcade tank
battles while replacing the flat battlefield with a stylized 3D city. Movement
is deliberately limited to four directions: this is a tactical arcade game,
not a free-roaming tank simulator.

## What is in this Alpha

- 35 stages for one player or local shared-camera two-player co-op.
- A fixed 45-degree view, local battlefield framing, and global minimap.
- Destructible offices, homes, shops, terrain, forests, and national bases.
- Shell-on-shell cancellation, multi-hit players, respawning, and visible
  direct-fire streaks.
- Nine pickup types, including the new conditional Bandage. Stars advance the
  selected vehicle one tier at a time; Shovel temporarily repairs and
  reinforces the base walls.
- Adjustable lives, player HP, enemy speed, fire frequency, and spawn pace.
- A classified end-of-stage K.O. report. Grenade bonus kills are intentionally
  excluded from the direct-fire tank totals.
- Procedural arcade rendering, animated tracks, shadows, impact effects,
  explosions, minimap alerts, and the inherited arcade audio-cue set.

## Three vehicle progressions

Each nation has four visually distinct WWII-era tiers. Every new life returns
with the player's current upgraded tier.

| Nation | Light | Medium | Heavy | Super-heavy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | M24 Chaffee | M4A3(76)W Sherman | M26 Pershing | T28/T95 |
| Soviet Union | T-70 | T-34-85 | IS-2 | KV-5 Project |
| Germany | Panzer II Ausf. F | Panzer IV Ausf. H | Tiger I Ausf. E | Maus |

The protected objective also changes with Player 1's nation: a Pentagon and
eagle, a Soviet ring fortress and monument, or a German parliament and
monument. These fictionalized historical elements provide period context and
do not express endorsement.

## Controls

| Context | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Menus | Arrow keys or `WASD` adjust; `Enter` or `Space` confirms |
| Player 1 | Arrow keys move; `Space`, Right Option, or Right Control fires |
| Player 2 | `WASD` moves; `F`, Left Option, or Left Control fires |
| In battle | `Enter` pauses; `Esc` returns to setup; `R` restarts |
| Display/stage | `F8` changes quality; `F11` toggles borderless mode; `N`/`B` changes stage |
| Exit | `Esc` or `Q` from the main setup screen |

## Alpha limitations

This app has an ad-hoc code signature for bundle-integrity checks. It is not
signed with an Apple Developer ID and is not notarized, so Gatekeeper may block
a downloaded copy. Clean-Mac Gatekeeper behavior and launch steps have not yet
been verified; do not disable Gatekeeper or other macOS security protections.
Full manual one-/two-player QA, long-session performance, and hardware audio
playback also remain to be recorded before publication.
